<p>Kind was founded in July 2014 and, from day one, we’ve collected data on our energy use, travel and purchases. Between 2014 and 2018 we offset more than 100% of our emissions through the World Land Trust Carbon Balanced Fund. In 2020 we joined the World Land Trust’s Carbon Balanced programme, and we’re now using its calculator to measure our footprint, starting with 2025 and working back to 2019. We’ll publish the results here, along with what we offset.</p>
